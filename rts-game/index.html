Scrapline Command

Gather scrap. Build your base. Destroy theirs.

Your mission

  • Deploy your base. You start with your Recycler on wheels. Drive it onto a glowing scrap geyser and press Deploy base. It becomes your headquarters and slowly pumps that geyser.
  • Collect scrap. Scavengers gather loose scrap automatically. Scrap pays for everything.
  • Upgrade your Recycler to Recycler II. Only its Scavenger IIs can deploy on geysers, and only its Constructor IIs can repair.
  • Claim more geysers. Send a Scavenger II to another geyser to turn it into an Extractor. Join an Extractor Silo onto it and its scrap flows straight into your total. Until then, a Transport hauls it home.
  • Mind your storage. You can only hold as much scrap as you have room for (300 to start). Build Base Silos next to your Recycler to hold more.
  • Protect your Recycler at all costs. If it falls, you lose. Destroy the enemy Recycler to win.

Basic controls

  • Left-click or drag a box to select. Right-click to move, attack or gather.
  • Scroll the map with the arrow keys, by touching the screen edge, or by clicking the minimap. Mouse wheel zooms.
  • Select your Constructor to build a Factory, then use the Factory to build tanks.
  • Tap one of your vehicles to select it. Then tap the map to move, tap an enemy to attack, or tap scrap to gather.
  • Drag one finger to scroll. Pinch to zoom. Press and hold, then drag to select a group.
  • The Army button selects all your fighters, or just Scouts, Tanks or Artillery. Tap the minimap to jump around.
  • Select your Constructor to build a Factory, then use the Factory to build tanks.
  • Tip: turn your phone sideways for the best view.

Difficulty

Pause the game to open Help (or press F1).

Tips

  • Scouts are fast and see far: send one to find the enemy base.
  • Artillery hits from very far, but only what your side can see. Pair it with scouts.
  • Destroyed vehicles leave salvage. Scavengers can collect it.
  • You can only build near your own buildings. Extractors on geysers push that area outward.
  • Loose scrap runs out. Geysers never do, so upgrade your Recycler and claim them early.
  • A Radar pings every 30 seconds, briefly revealing a wide area. Artillery can fire at anything the ping shows.
  • Research at a Research Lab, then upgrade a Factory to Factory II to build Mk II vehicles.
  • The scrap counter shows what you hold and how much room you have. When it turns yellow you're full, and scrap waits outside until you spend some or build a Base Silo.
  • Transports are unarmed but shielded. The shield recharges a few seconds after they stop taking hits.
  • The enemy attacks in waves. Towers and a few tanks at home keep your Recycler safe while your army is away.
  • Constructors can repair damaged buildings: select one and tap or right-click the building.
