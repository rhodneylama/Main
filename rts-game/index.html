Scrapline Command

A top-down battle for scrap, flags and survival.

Your mission

  • Collect scrap. Scavengers gather it automatically. Scrap pays for everything.
  • Capture flags. Park vehicles inside a flag's circle. Flags give income, let you build nearby, and drain enemy tickets.
  • Win by draining the enemy's tickets to zero or destroying their Recycler (their HQ).

Basic controls

  • Left-click or drag a box to select. Right-click to move, attack or gather.
  • Scroll the map with the arrow keys, by touching the screen edge, or by clicking the minimap. Mouse wheel zooms.
  • Select your Constructor to build a Factory, then use the Factory to build tanks.

Difficulty

Press F1 in game for all controls.

Tips

  • Scouts are fast and see far: send them to grab flags early.
  • Artillery hits from very far, but only what your side can see. Pair it with scouts.
  • Destroyed vehicles leave salvage. Scavengers can collect it.
  • You can only build near your buildings or flags you hold. Capturing flags expands your territory.
  • Every combat vehicle you lose costs 1 ticket. Holding more flags than the enemy drains theirs.
