How to play

Your mission

  • Deploy your base. You start with your Recycler on wheels. Drive it onto a glowing scrap geyser and press Deploy base. It becomes your headquarters and slowly pumps that geyser.
  • Collect scrap. Scavengers gather loose scrap automatically. Scrap pays for everything.
  • Upgrade your Recycler to Recycler II. Only its Scavenger IIs can deploy on geysers, and only its Constructor IIs can repair.
  • Claim more geysers. Send a Scavenger II to another geyser to turn it into an Extractor. Its scrap flows straight into your total.
  • Mind your storage. You can only hold as much scrap as you have room for (300 to start). Build Base Silos next to your Recycler to hold more.
  • Protect your Recycler at all costs. If it falls, you lose. Destroy the enemy Recycler to win.

Factions

This manual uses Scrapline names. The Kethra Concord fights by exactly the same rules under its own names: Recycler = Nexus (on wheels: Monad), Scavenger = Skimmer, Constructor = Loom, Scout = Shard, Hornet = Talon, Tank = Bulwark, Sentry = Pylon, Artillery = Spire, Factory = Assembler, Extractor = Siphon, Base Silo = Vault Orb, Gun Tower = Prism Tower, Research Lab = Archive, Radar = Sensor Array, Repair Pad = Mending Lattice.

Vehicles

  • Scout: fast, sees far, light gun. Hornet: light rocket tank between a Scout and a Tank: much faster and cheaper than a Tank, less armour, hard-hitting rockets.
  • Tank: tough, steady damage. Sentry: a purely defensive turret on tracks. Drive it into place and press Deploy: it digs in and fires a rapid stream at anything in range, never moving or chasing. Pack up (or any move order) to move it again; packed up it cannot fire.
  • Artillery: huge range and splash, but must stop to fire and only hits what your side can see.

Tips

  • Scouts are fast and see far: send one to find the enemy base.
  • Artillery hits from very far, but only what your side can see. Pair it with scouts.
  • Destroyed vehicles leave salvage. Scavengers can collect it.
  • You can only build near your own buildings. Extractors on geysers push that area outward.
  • Loose scrap runs out. Geysers never do, so upgrade your Recycler and claim them early.
  • A Radar pings every 30 seconds, briefly revealing a wide area. Artillery can fire at anything the ping shows.
  • Research at a Research Lab, then upgrade a Factory to Factory II to build Mk II vehicles.
  • The scrap counter shows what you hold and how much room you have. When it turns yellow you're full, and scrap waits outside until you spend some or build a Base Silo.
  • The enemy attacks in waves. Towers and a few tanks at home keep your Recycler safe while your army is away.
  • Constructors can repair damaged buildings: select one and tap or right-click the building.
  • Save from the pause screen (three slots, plus an autosave every 3 minutes and whenever you leave the page). Load game is on the pause and start screens. You can also download a save as a file.
  • Call-in (top bar): a Supply drop (300 scrap at your Recycler) or Reinforcements (2 Tanks and 2 Hornets where you tap). You get one per battle; every uplink you hold earns another every 2 minutes.
  • Uplinks (capture and hold battles): park combat vehicles by an uplink mast to capture it. Once they're online (5:00), hold two of the three for 3 minutes to win.
  • Campaign landings: whoever holds a battlefield has a base there. Attacking, pick a landing zone: an unclaimed geyser, or one of theirs to liberate (destroy the Extractor, then deploy on its geyser). Supply crates come down with you.
  • Campaign: fight a war across fifteen battlefields. On the war map, tap a battlefield. Attack an enemy one next to yours, or join a battle your side is fighting: Take command of the allied base and army, or Reinforce with a strike force (gold markers) while the allied commander runs the base and sends you every third vehicle it builds. Each neighbouring battlefield your side holds adds vehicles to your start. Leave battle on the pause screen hands the fight back to the allied commander.
  • Music follows the battle: it builds up when fighting breaks out near your forces. Turn it on or off on the start or pause screen.
